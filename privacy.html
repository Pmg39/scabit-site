Privacy Policy — Scab It

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Scab It is a construction calculator, measuring, and blueprint-viewing app for carpenters and builders. Your privacy is simple to explain: Scab It does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

No data collection

Scab It has no account system, no login, and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, or any other personal information.

Everything stays on your device

Your calculations, saved jobs, measurements, photos, and any blueprints you open are stored only on your device. The developer never receives, sees, or has access to them.

Camera

Scab It uses your device's camera only for its on-device augmented-reality measuring feature. Camera images are processed live on your device to estimate distances and are not recorded, transmitted, or collected. Any photos you choose to save are kept on your device only.

No tracking or analytics

Scab It contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking technologies, and no third-party software-development kits that collect data.

No internet required

Scab It works fully offline and does not send your information anywhere.

Children's privacy

Scab It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Removing your data

Because all data is stored locally, deleting the app removes all of it from your device.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
