Scab It is the carpenter's pocket toolbox: jobsite calculators (concrete, drywall, rafters, stairs, layout and more), AR camera measuring, a level, and a DXF/PDF blueprint viewer — all offline, no account needed.

Need help or found a bug?
Email pmgtech099@gmail.com and I'll get back to you. Please include your device model and iOS version, and a quick note on what happened — screenshots help.

A few quick tips

  • Imperial or metric: toggle at the top of most tools. Imperial fields accept feet-inch fractions like 4 3/4.
  • Entering runs / depths: each length has a feet box and an inch box — put inches in the right-hand box.
  • Saving a job: most tools have Save / Open to store and reload your work.
  • AR measuring: move the phone slowly to let it map the space before measuring; good light helps accuracy.
